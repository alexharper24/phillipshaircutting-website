Our Story

A Family Trade Since 1951

Phillips Haircutting started with founder James Phillips and has been passed down through the family ever since, and is now run by owner Scot Phillips alongside barbers Taylor and JT.

Barbering here has always been about more than haircuts. It's the conversation, the regulars who've come in for decades, and the pride of sending every client out looking their best. Old fashioned, but modern where it counts.
