Our Story

Old Fashioned,
But Modern

Phillips Haircutting was founded by James Phillips in 1951 and has stayed in the family through three generations of barbers. Today it's owned by his descendant Scot Phillips, who keeps the same standards his family built the shop on.

We've never chased trends for their own sake. What's kept people coming back for decades is simple: a first-class cut, a fair price, and a barber who actually knows your name. Some of our regulars have been sitting in these chairs since the 1990s — and now they bring their kids and parents too.

Walk in and you'll get the same welcome the shop has offered since day one. That's the part we'll never modernize.
