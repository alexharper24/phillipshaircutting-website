Cuts & Grooming

What We Offer

Classic Haircut

A full, custom haircut: scissor or clipper work, tapered neckline, styled and finished the way you like it.

Skin & Taper Fade

Low, mid, high, or bald fades blended clean into your length for a crisp, modern look.

Beard Trim & Maintenance

Shaping, trimming, and upkeep to keep your beard looking sharp and even.

Straight-Razor Line-Up

Hot-towel straight-razor detailing on your beard and edges for the sharpest possible finish.

Kids' Cuts

Friendly, patient cuts for kids of all ages. First haircuts are always welcome.

Military & Buzz Cuts

Regulation cuts and clean buzz cuts, done quick and done right.

Custom Designs

Hard parts, designs, and detail work. Bring a photo or an idea and your barber will dial it in.

Seniors' Cuts

A comfortable, unhurried cut for our older regulars, a shop staple for over 70 years.

Honest, Old-Fashioned Prices

Our regulars will tell you the prices are some of the best around. Because rates can change, we keep them current in-shop rather than out of date online, so call or ask your barber for today's pricing.
